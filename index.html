01 / ABOUT

Imagination with intention.

I take the work seriously. Myself? A little less so.

I've spent much of my life exploring the space between imagination and possibility—and discovering what can happen when curiosity, creativity, and the right people come together.

I'm a storyteller, artist, entrepreneur, and enthusiastic collaborator with an appetite for unconventional thinking. I enjoy challenging assumptions, finding connections where others might not see them, and exploring ideas from angles that aren't immediately obvious.

My career has taken me through music, animation, advertising, brand development, creative production, and entrepreneurship. Along the way, I've built businesses, developed original concepts, worked with extraordinary artists, and learned that great creative work requires more than inspiration. It takes perspective, communication, adaptability, and the ability to bring people together around a shared vision.

I believe the most interesting possibilities often emerge when different minds, disciplines, and experiences collide. That's where I feel most at home: in the middle of a good conversation, an ambitious creative challenge, or an idea that makes everyone in the room lean forward.
