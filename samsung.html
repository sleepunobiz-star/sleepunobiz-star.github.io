HI HEY
HELLO.

How an original song by The Chicharones found its way into Samsung’s global Galaxy S4 campaign.

Original musicCreative pitchingBrand collaborationSamsung Galaxy S4
The opportunity

An idea worth pitching.

Christopher Tafoya, known as Sleep of Oldominion, co-created and performed “Hi Hey Hello” as part of hip-hop duo The Chicharones.

He saw an opportunity for the song beyond a traditional record release: its energy and personality could work in a telecommunications advertisement. He proactively pitched that possibility to advertising agency Leo Burnett.

Leo Burnett subsequently brought the song to Samsung, and it became part of the international Galaxy S4 campaign.

From music to global brand

The creative journey.

01 / ORIGINAL WORK

The song.

“Hi Hey Hello” was created and performed by The Chicharones, the duo of Christopher Tafoya and Josh Martinez.

02 / INITIATIVE

The pitch.

Christopher recognized the song’s advertising potential and approached Leo Burnett with the idea.

03 / PLACEMENT

The campaign.

The agency presented the song to Samsung; it was used in the Galaxy S4 campaign, reaching an international audience.

Role & attribution

The right credits matter.

Christopher Tafoya / The Chicharones: original song, performance, and proactive advertising pitch to Leo Burnett.

Leo Burnett and Joseph Kahn: campaign creative direction and production. Christopher does not claim to have directed the Samsung advertisement.

This project illustrates the ability to connect an original creative asset with a commercial opportunity, advocate for an idea, and collaborate across music and advertising.

The account of the initial pitch is provided by Christopher Tafoya. Samsung’s official announcement independently identifies The Chicharones in connection with the campaign.
